Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This policy explains what information 3steez.com collects, why, and what happens to it. This site is operated by Trés Styles (3Steez), a DJ and MC based in Ann Arbor, Michigan.

Short version: the only information collected is what you type into the booking form, and it is used to reply to you. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Information you give us

If you submit the booking form, you provide:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address and phone number
  • Your company or event name
  • Details about your event, such as location, expected attendance and what you are looking for
  • How you heard about 3Steez

That information is used for one purpose: to respond to your inquiry and, if you book, to plan and perform the work. It is not sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

Information collected automatically

This site does not run analytics software, advertising pixels, or visitor tracking, and it does not set cookies of its own.

Like any website, it is delivered by a hosting provider and a content delivery network (Cloudflare), which process standard technical request data such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request in order to serve pages and protect against abuse.

Embedded video

Some pages embed videos from YouTube. When a page with an embedded video loads or you press play, YouTube may set its own cookies and receive data about your visit under Google's privacy policy, not this one. If you would rather avoid that, do not interact with the embedded players.

Links to other sites

This site links to services such as Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube and ticketing or RSVP pages. Once you follow a link, that service's own privacy policy governs what happens there.

How long information is kept

Booking inquiries are kept as long as needed to respond and to maintain normal business records. You can ask for your inquiry to be deleted at any time.

Your choices

You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Use the booking page to get in touch and say what you would like done.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the booking page.
